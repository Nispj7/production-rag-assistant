Quantum Computing and RAG Systems

Quantum computing is a rapidly-emerging technology that harnesses the laws of quantum mechanics to solve problems too complex for classical computers. 
Quantum mechanics is the branch of physics that studies behavior at the atomic and subatomic levels.

Key concepts in quantum computing include:
1. Superposition: The ability of a quantum system to be in multiple states at the same time.
2. Entanglement: A quantum phenomenon where particles become interconnected, such that the state of one instantly influences the state of another, regardless of distance.
3. Qubits: The basic unit of quantum information, analogous to the bit in classical computing.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is an architectural pattern that improves the accuracy and reliability of Large Language Model (LLM) responses.
By retrieving facts from an external knowledge base (like a vector store containing documentation about quantum mechanics), the LLM can reference verified sources before generating answers.
This significantly reduces hallucinations and ensures context-specific accuracy.
